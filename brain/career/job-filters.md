# Job filters (the Sales Agent reads this before every job-search)

> Starter template. Lines marked `{{…}}` are placeholders the CEO must fill. If a placeholder affects a decision (e.g. rate floor), the Sales Agent asks the CEO instead of guessing.

## Target roles
- Shopify / Shopify Liquid Developer (OS 2.0 themes, sections, speed, CRO pages)
- Webflow Developer (builds, CMS, interactions)
- WordPress Developer (Elementor, WooCommerce, ACF, fixes/maintenance)
- Front-end Developer (HTML/CSS/JS), Figma-to-code
- Wix Developer, funnel builders (Checkout Champ, Funnelish) — lower priority
- Full-stack / AI-assisted web dev (React/Next, Supabase) — case by case
- Not a fit: custom Shopify app/Admin-API app builds, Shopify Plus administration (Flow/scripts/B2B), store operations/VA roles, logistics, video-ad editing, SEO-only log analysis.

## Employment
- Types: full-time, part-time, contract, gig.
- Remote only (or "work from anywhere").
- Hours: CEO is in Davao, PH (UTC+8) and can cover US, AU, UK or CA business hours.

## Freshness & competition
- Posted within the last 14 days (older still-open posts allowed for thin categories like Webflow, flagged as "older").
- Skip posts with a clear signal of 100+ applicants, or no clear apply method.
- Employer signals to prefer: long "member since", several prior posts, clear scope, a real company site.

## Budget per run
- Target 10–15 strong leads per run, as a numbered, prioritized queue with ★ on the best.
- OnlineJobs.ph apply points: 1 point per application; points available this run: `{{APPLY_POINTS}}` (CEO confirms; reserve unless told to spend down).

## Red flags → skip, or mark "manual" for the CEO
- Unpaid test work over ~1 hour; upfront fees; crypto/gift-card pay; early requests for government ID.
- Cloning another company's site or landing page pixel-for-pixel (copyright).
- "Don't use AI for answers" or assessments needing the CEO's own words → manual.
- Loom/video intro, external forms (Jotform, Google Forms, ATS), apply-by-email → manual, list exactly what's needed.
- Demands for publishable A/B test numbers, before/after speed results, references, hardware/internet specs → manual (never fabricate).
- Scope wildly underpriced (e.g. full SaaS for a landing-page budget) → skip with reason.
- Language requirement other than English/Filipino.
