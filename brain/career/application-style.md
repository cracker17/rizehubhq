# Application style (the Sales Agent writes every application to this)

> Starter template. The CEO adds winning examples at the bottom. Portfolio links, resume links, experience claims and the signature block come ONLY from `brain/career/portfolio.md`.

## Register
- Confident, peer-to-peer, warm but never desperate. Lead with what the CEO brings to their problem, not what he wants.
- Show you read the post: reference their product, stack, niche or exact wording.
- Banned phrases: "kindly consider me", "sir/ma'am", "I am writing to apply", "I hope this finds you well", "hardworking and dedicated", "I am the perfect candidate", "please give me a chance".

## Structure
1. **Opening (1–2 sentences):** a specific technical insight about their stack/problem, a short concrete story, or the direct answer to their first screening question. Never a summary of experience.
2. **Proof:** 2–3 sentences tying real experience to their need; one concrete detail from portfolio.md.
3. **Screening answers:** numbered, in their order, direct.
4. **Links:** 3–5 portfolio links matching the job's platform (never the full list) + the matching resume link.
5. **Logistics:** availability, timezone (mention Davao, PH when location/timezone matters; can cover US/AU/UK hours), rate per job-filters.md.
6. **Close:** low-friction offer, e.g. "Happy to do a free mini-audit of your current theme and send 3 fixes, no strings," plus a simple next step.
7. **Signature:** exactly as in portfolio.md.

## Length by source
| Source | Words | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| OnlineJobs.ph | 200–250 | Warmer; subject line field; no file upload, links in body; each message individually tailored (copy-paste mass applying risks a ban) |
| Indeed / Seek | 150–200 | Subject that gets opened |
| LinkedIn / email | ≤ 150 | Crisper, one link cluster |
| Upwork | ≤ 180 | First 2 lines show in preview: make them the hook |

## Subject lines
Specific and outcome-led, e.g. "Shopify dev: OS 2.0 sections, can start this week". Never "Job Application". Include any subject code the post requires, exactly.

## Honesty rules
- Experience claim: use only the figure in portfolio.md (`{{YEARS_CLAIM}}`, CEO confirms).
- Never invent projects, clients, metrics, before/after numbers or tools.
- If the post asks for something not in portfolio.md, say so in one line and show the nearest real equivalent. Keep disclosures consistent across follow-ups (log them in job_tracker).
- Starter list of gaps to disclose, not claim (CEO to confirm/edit): Shopify Plus admin (Flow/scripts/B2B); custom Shopify apps; Replo, GoHighLevel, Bricks/Zion builders; end-to-end subscription (Recharge/Skio) builds; publishable A/B or before/after conversion numbers.

## Reusable technical hooks (use only when relevant to the post)
- Cloned sections depend on the source theme's CSS/JS; rebuild as a real OS 2.0 section with its own schema.
- Collection pages dropping from the index because canonicals carry sort/filter params.
- Duplicate Product JSON-LD from theme + SEO app disagreeing on price/availability.
- "Broken" contact forms are often PHP mail() from a blacklisted shared IP; check the network tab first.
- Webflow sites fail at the class system, not the design.
