# QA checklist: outreach-draft

1. **Finding verified**: the finding in each message is in lead notes and visible on the site today (spot-check 5 or all if < 10). Verify: `web_fetch`/`pagespeed`.
2. **One finding only** per message. Verify: read.
3. **Names/URLs correct**: company, person and URL match the lead record. Verify: `rizehub_readonly`.
4. **Length**: email ≤ 90 words, DM ≤ 60 words; subject ≤ 45 chars. Verify: count.
5. **First line about them**, not about RizeHub. Verify: read.
6. **One CTA** (question). Verify: read.
7. **No invented proof**: no stats, results, testimonials, case studies, prices. Verify: read + brain search.
8. **Opt-out line** on every email. Verify: read.
9. **Not suppressed / not contacted before**. Verify: lead stage + suppression list.
10. **Spam-safe**: no caps shouting, "guaranteed", "act now", no attachments, ≤ 1 link. Verify: read.
11. **Tone** matches `brand-voice.md`. Verify: compare.
12. **Nothing sent**: emails only as pending approvals; DMs drafts only. Verify: activity log.
