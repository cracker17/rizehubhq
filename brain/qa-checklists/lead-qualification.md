# QA checklist: lead-qualification

1. **All five factors scored** 0–2 with a total out of 10. Verify: read card.
2. **Evidence per score**: each score cites a quote, form field or verified site fact. Verify: compare to source.
3. **Unknowns marked**: nothing unstated is scored as known. Verify: compare card to thread.
4. **Math correct**: total equals sum. Verify: arithmetic.
5. **Route matches band** (8–10 pipeline, 5–7 discovery, 0–4 nurture/decline) or deviation explained. Verify: read.
6. **Platform verified** with fingerprint when a site exists. Verify: `web_fetch`.
7. **Ask quoted** in the lead's own words. Verify: compare.
8. **Next step** is one concrete action. Verify: read.
9. **Escalations**: existing clients, complaints, legal/press flagged to CEO, not scored. Verify: read.
10. **Data minimised**: no sensitive personal data recorded. Verify: read.
11. **Nothing sent or changed** in RizeHub. Verify: activity log.
