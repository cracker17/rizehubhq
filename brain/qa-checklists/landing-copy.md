# QA checklist: landing-copy

Severity: C = critical (auto-fail), M = major, m = minor.

1. **Sections present (M).** Hero, offer, benefits, proof (or explicit "omitted: no proof supplied"), FAQ, final CTA exist per brief. Verify: scan labels.
2. **Hero headline (M).** H1 ≤ 10 words, states a benefit; subhead ≤ 25 words. Verify: count.
3. **One H1 (M).** Exactly one H1; keyword in H1 or first H2 if the brief sets a keyword.
4. **Title/meta (M).** Title ≤ 60, meta ≤ 155 characters.
5. **Offer exact (C).** Price, terms, dates, inclusions match the brief word for word. Verify: compare.
6. **Proof real (C).** Every testimonial, number, logo, guarantee and credential traces to the brief or client site; no invented reviews or scarcity.
7. **CTA consistency (M).** All CTAs point to the same briefed destination; long pages have 3+ CTA placements; CTAs are verb + outcome.
8. **Objections handled (M).** 4–6 FAQ items answer real objections (price, trust, timeline, returns/guarantee).
9. **Framework stated (m).** PAS or AIDA named and followed in order.
10. **Variants (m).** 2 alternate headlines provided.
11. **Schema (M).** JSON-LD parses and matches visible copy; no fake ratings.
12. **Voice and readability (m).** Matches `brand.md`; no hype words from SOP list; sentences mostly under 20 words.
13. **Compliance (C when applicable).** Health, income or result claims are substantiated or removed; material connections disclosed.
