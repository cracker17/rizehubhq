# QA checklist: social-captions

Severity: C = critical (auto-fail), M = major, m = minor.

1. **Count (C).** Number of captions equals the brief, per platform. Verify: count blocks.
2. **Hook length (M).** First line ≤ 12 words on every caption. Verify: word count.
3. **Keyword placement (M).** Stated keyword appears in the first 125 characters. Verify: character position.
4. **Platform limits (M).** IG ≤ 2,200, Threads ≤ 500, LinkedIn ≤ 3,000 characters, Shorts title ≤ 100. Verify: character count.
5. **One CTA (M).** Exactly one call to action per caption. Verify: read.
6. **Hashtag count (m).** Within SOP ranges per platform; none generic or unrelated. Verify: count and read.
7. **No invented facts (C).** Every number, review, price, discount, timeframe and credential matches the brief or client site. Verify: compare.
8. **Disclosure (C).** Sponsored/gifted/affiliate posts carry #ad or Paid partnership note at the start.
9. **No prohibited claims (C).** No unsupported health, weight-loss, income or guaranteed-result claims.
10. **Alt text (m).** Every image post has alt text ≤ 125 characters.
11. **Voice (m).** Matches `brand.md`; no banned phrases; no more than one em dash per caption.
12. **Variety (m).** No two captions in the batch open with the same first 3 words.
13. **Links and handles (M).** Every link and @handle is real or a marked placeholder. Verify: `web_fetch` links.
14. **Branding (M).** No personal names or emails unless the brief says so.
