# QA checklist: short-video-script

Severity: C = critical (auto-fail), M = major, m = minor.

1. **Length (M).** Final timestamp equals target length ±3 s. Verify: last row of beat sheet.
2. **Hook timing (M).** Hook visual and text land in 0–2 s. Verify: first row.
3. **Timed beats (M).** Every row has a time range, visual, on-screen text and VO/audio cell (VO may be "none"). Verify: scan.
4. **VO fits (M).** VO word count ≤ seconds × 2.5. Verify: count words.
5. **Sound-off ready (M).** Every key message appears as on-screen text.
6. **Safe zones (m).** Script states text placement inside safe zones (not top 14% / bottom 20% / right column).
7. **One CTA (M).** Single CTA, spoken and on screen, in the last 5 s.
8. **Shootable (M).** Shot list and props/assets list present; no shot requires footage the brief says is unavailable.
9. **No invented facts (C).** Claims, results, reviews and prices trace to the brief. Verify: compare.
10. **Consent and disclosure (C).** Real people's voice/likeness has confirmed consent; sponsored content has a disclosure.
11. **Licensed audio (C).** No copyrighted songs named for use; music described as licensed library track.
12. **Caption included (M).** Caption, hashtags and cover text present and pass social-captions limits.
13. **Branding (M).** Client or RizeHub branding only; no personal names or emails unless the brief says so.
