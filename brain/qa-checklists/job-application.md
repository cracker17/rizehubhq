# QA checklist: job-application

1. **Post live** at time of drafting. Verify: `web_fetch`.
2. **Every screening question answered**, in order. Verify: list questions vs answers.
3. **Required keyword / subject code / opening word** present exactly. Verify: search text.
4. **Word count** fits source (OJ.ph 200–250, Indeed 150–200, LinkedIn/email ≤ 150). Verify: count.
5. **Hook**: first sentence is a specific insight about their stack/problem or an answer to their question, not a self-introduction. Verify: read.
6. **3–5 portfolio links**, all same platform as the job, copied exactly from `portfolio.md`. Verify: compare.
7. **Correct resume link** for the role type. Verify: compare.
8. **No invented experience**: every claim (years, projects, tools, numbers) exists in `portfolio.md`. Verify: search portfolio.md.
9. **Banned phrases absent** (kindly consider, sir/ma'am, etc. per application-style.md). Verify: search.
10. **Rate** matches job-filters.md or their posted rate, consistent with prior quotes in job_tracker. Verify: compare.
11. **Low-friction offer + signature** present. Verify: read.
12. **Tracker** status `drafted`, not `applied`; nothing submitted. Verify: job_tracker + activity log.
