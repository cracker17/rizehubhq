# Ideal client profile (who the Sales Agent targets)

## Target
- **Where:** US-based businesses first; Australia, the UK and Canada when the brief says so.
- **What they need:** Shopify, Webflow or WordPress development; UI/UX or graphic design; SEO and content.
- **Who:** small and mid-size businesses with a live site and a person who decides (owner, founder, marketing lead):
  - Shopify / DTC brands (skincare, apparel, home, food & drink, fitness, pets) with a real catalog and ad or review activity
  - Service businesses on WordPress (clinics, law firms, trades, agencies) whose site is slow, dated or broken
  - B2B SaaS and studios on Webflow that are hiring a developer or shipping marketing pages often
- **Buying triggers (verify on the live site before using):** slow mobile pages (LCP > 2.5 s), low mobile PageSpeed, broken pages or links, missing SSL on forms, outdated theme, a developer job post, an upcoming launch or season.

## Fit score (0–100)
Need (a verified problem we fix) 40 · ability to pay (catalog, ads, team, reviews) 25 · service match 20 · reachable through a **published business contact** 15. Drop anything under 50.

## Not a fit (skip, do not contact)
- No public business contact (never guess addresses, never use data brokers, never scrape LinkedIn or personal profiles).
- Consumers / individuals, minors, anyone who opted out (suppression list), existing RizeHub clients, competitors.
- Adult, gambling, crypto schemes, supplements with medical claims, weapons, anything illegal.
- Platforms/services we don't do (see services.md).

## Compliance
CAN-SPAM for US leads (real sender, postal address, working opt-out, honour it forever); AU Spam Act and UK PECR when targeting those countries (ask Julev before the first AU/UK campaign: they have stricter consent rules for some recipients); PH Data Privacy Act for what we store (business data only, minimum needed).
