# SOP: short-video-script

Owner: writer. Output: a shootable script for Reels, TikTok or Shorts (15–60 s) that the client's creator or editor can shoot and cut without questions.

## Inputs to confirm
Client, platform, target length, goal, product/service facts, who appears on camera (founder, creator, voiceover only), available footage, offer and CTA, disclosure needs. A real person's voice or likeness: written consent must be confirmed in the brief, else `ask_ceo`.

## Steps
1. Read client `brand.md` and any approved past videos.
2. Pick one format: demo, problem→solution, myth vs fact, POV, listicle, before/after (real only), behind-the-scenes, founder story, FAQ answer, UGC-style testimonial (only with a real customer's permission and words).
3. Write 3 hooks. The hook must work visually and in text within 0–2 s, even with sound off. Choose one.
4. Build the beat sheet with timings. Keep one idea per 2–4 s shot. Pattern interrupt every 3–5 s (cut, zoom, text change).
5. Write VO or dialogue that fits the time: about 2.5 words per second.
6. Add on-screen text for every key line (sound-off viewing), inside safe zones: keep text out of the top 14% and bottom 20% of a 9:16 frame and away from the right-side button column.
7. End with one CTA, spoken and on screen. Loop-friendly endings help replays.
8. Add audio direction: "licensed commercial-library track, upbeat, 110–120 BPM" rather than naming copyrighted songs. Note SFX.
9. Write the caption (follow `brain/sops/social-captions.md`) and a cover/thumbnail text (≤ 5 words).
10. Self-QA against `brain/qa-checklists/short-video-script.md`, then `submit_output`.


## Rules
- Word count of VO ≤ length in seconds × 2.5.
- No fake testimonials, actors posing as customers without a disclosure, or invented results.
- No voice cloning or imitation of a real person without written consent.
- Licensed music, fonts and footage only.
