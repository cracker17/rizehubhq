# SOP: content-calendar

Owner: writer. Output: a calendar file the CEO can approve and the client can execute. We never schedule or post.

## Inputs to confirm
Client, platforms, period (dates), cadence per platform, goal (awareness, traffic, sales, leads), offers/launches with exact terms and dates, handles, links, time zone, existing content or assets. Missing offer terms, dates or links → `ask_ceo`. Everything else missing → `[PLACEHOLDER: ...]`.

## Steps
1. Read `brain/clients/<slug>/profile.md`, `brand.md`, past approved posts, and this client's last social report if one exists (`rizehub_reports`, read only).
2. Set 3–5 content pillars. For each: purpose, funnel stage, share of posts (e.g. Educate 35%, Product/Service 25%, Proof 20%, Behind-the-scenes 10%, Community 10%). Proof posts only if real proof exists.
3. Map key dates: launches, sales, holidays in the client's market (US, AU or UK, not PH), industry events. Only dates you can verify.
4. Set a cadence the client can sustain. Defaults if the brief is silent: Instagram 3–4/week (2 Reels, 1 carousel, 1 static), TikTok 3/week, LinkedIn 3/week, Threads 5/week, Facebook 3/week, Shorts 2/week. Stories daily is optional.
5. Suggest posting slots in the client's time zone, with a note that slots should be adjusted to the account's own insights after 2–4 weeks.
6. Fill one row per post. Build around 1–2 hero pieces per week and repurpose them across platforms.
7. Write hooks for every row. No duplicate hooks. Mix formats: question, number, POV, myth, story, demo.
8. Add a measurement line: which metric each pillar is judged on (saves, shares, comments, clicks, watch time).
9. Self-QA against `brain/qa-checklists/content-calendar.md`, then `submit_output`.

## Calendar template (CSV or markdown table)
| Date | Day | Time (TZ) | Platform | Format | Pillar | Topic | Hook | CTA | Asset needed | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-06 | Mon | 11:00 ET | IG Reel | 20s demo | Product | 3 ways to style the bundle | "One bundle, three outfits, zero stress." | Shop link in bio | Model video, 3 looks | Client team | Draft |

Status values: Draft, Needs asset, Ready for CEO. Asset needs become separate tasks (graphic, video) for the COO.

## Also include in the file
- Pillar table with percentages and goals.
- Key dates list with sources.
- Repurposing map: hero piece → derivatives.
- Placeholders list and open questions.
- Compliance notes: #ad or Paid partnership for sponsored/gifted posts; claims needing proof flagged.

## Example hook mix for one week (DTC)
- "Stop buying 3 separate serums." (myth/problem)
- "POV: your order arrives in 2 days." (only if shipping time is confirmed)
- "What's actually inside our [product]." (transparency)
