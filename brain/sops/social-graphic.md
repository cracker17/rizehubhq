# SOP: social-graphic

Owner: graphic-1 / graphic-2 (templates and carousels → graphic-2). Output: organic social posts, carousels, story frames, or reusable templates.

## 1. Inputs (missing → `ask_ceo`)
- Platform(s), format (single, carousel, story), count, dates if part of a content calendar.
- Approved captions/on-image copy from Social Marketing. Client `brand.md` and assets.

## 2. Specs
- Instagram/Facebook feed: 1080×1350 (4:5) preferred, 1080×1080 allowed. Grid preview crops to 3:4 center; keep key content in the centre 1080×1440 area of 4:5.
- Carousel: 2–10 slides, same size, slide 1 = hook, last slide = CTA/save/share prompt, visual continuity across slides.
- Stories: 1080×1920, top 250 px and bottom 340 px clear.
- LinkedIn: 1200×1200 or 1080×1350; X: 1600×900; Pinterest: 1000×1500.

## 3. Design rules
- One idea per post; on-image text ≤ 20 words (carousel ≤ 35 per slide), headline ≥ 56 px.
- Brand fonts/colours only; contrast ≥ 4.5:1; consistent logo/handle placement.
- Photography: client-owned, licensed stock, or `image_gen` scenes checked for artefacts; never AI-generated text, people who look like real individuals, or fake product.
- Accessible: provide alt text for each graphic in the output notes.

## 4. Templates (when requested)
- Build in HTML/SVG or Figma-ready layout with locked brand zones and named editable slots (`{headline}`, `{subtext}`, `{image}`).
- Include 2 filled examples and a usage note: max characters per slot, image crop, do/don't.

## 5. Checks
- Dimensions exact (`identify`), text inside safe zones, spelling verified against the approved copy word for word.
- Preview every post at 375 px wide; readable in 2 s.
- No claims, stats, testimonials or prices beyond the approved copy.

## 6. Export and submit
PNG/JPG sRGB, `<client>_<yyyymmdd>_<topic>_<n>.png`; carousel slides numbered `_s01`. `submit_output` with files, contact sheet, alt text list, prompt log, licences, criteria_map. Posting or scheduling is a `request_external_action`.
